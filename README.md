## `> about_me`

I’m a final-year Computer Science student at Uganda Christian University, based in Uganda and expecting to graduate in July 2027. I’m interested in full-stack development and in building AI tools that are useful outside the classroom.

I completed a software development internship with the Business Automation Department at the Bank of Uganda in June 2026. I also took part in UCU’s Computer Science Recess Workshop from May to July 2025.
